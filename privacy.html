Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What this application is

"Canales YouTube" is a private tool used only by the owner of The Abyss Log to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Your data is also handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

Data we access and why

  • Permission to upload and manage videos and captions on the authorized channel, used only to publish the channel's own content.
  • An OAuth access token issued by Google, used only to call the YouTube Data API on behalf of that channel.

We do not access, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

Storage, sharing and retention

The authorization token is stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and is never shared, sold or transferred to anyone. No other personal data is stored. The token is deleted when access is revoked.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking of any kind.
